About Blood Unit Requests

CNPH Blood Bank accepts requests from in-patient wards, external hospitals, and registered medical institutions. All requests must be accompanied by licensed physician authorization and originating from registered health facilities. For questions about institutional registration or credential verification, contact CNPH Blood Bank administration.
